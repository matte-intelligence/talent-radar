# Talent Radar — product concept

**Version:** 0.9 (public) · **Date:** 2026-10-09 · **Owner:** Matte Intelligence Inc.

## Problem

Executive search research is still manual: days of scattered searching, notes
without provenance, shortlists without documented evidence. Buyers cannot see
*why* a candidate is on the list; candidates get judged on summaries they never
saw. Meanwhile AI tools generate plausible text with no accountability.

## Product

Talent Radar is a research tool for executive-search teams:

1. **Define the search** — mandate and role criteria, structured with the client.
2. **Gather authorized sources** — public and permitted market information,
   collected with provenance; no scraping of private data.
3. **Draft profiles** — Claude-API synthesis: source-linked profiles,
   role-requirement comparisons, draft reports.
4. **Verify & compare** — a professional checks every element against its source;
   flagged items require resolution.
5. **Signed recommendation** — the shortlist is reviewed and **signed** by a
   partner. The signature is the product: accountability you can inspect.

## Users

- **Buyer:** CEOs, boards, HR directors engaging executive search.
- **Operator:** research consultants at boutique firms (our parent firm's
  practice is the first design partner).
- **Not a user-facing product:** candidates never interact with the tool
  directly; all candidate-facing communication stays human.

## Positioning

People × Science × AI. The parent firm's 38 years of executive-search judgement
is the distribution and the evaluation bench; the AI platform is the
multiplier — not a replacement for either.
