## What it is

Executive research today starts from a blank page: hours of scattered searching,
results with no provenance, judgement with no documented evidence. Talent Radar
starts every mandate from a **living, documented map** and ends it with a
**signed human decision**.

### The planned workflow

```
 HUMAN        Define the search
              mandate and role criteria, set with the client
                 │
 AI · Claude   Gather authorized sources
              public, permitted market information, kept with its sources
                 │
 AI · Claude   Draft profiles
              synthesis and role comparison, prepared for review
                 │
 HUMAN        Verify & compare
              a professional checks every element against its source
                 │
 HUMAN        Signed recommendation
              the shortlist is reviewed and signed by a partner
```

**AI assists, humans decide** — the operating principle of everything we ship.

## Privacy & responsible-AI principles

- Career data is **never** used to train public models.
- Physiological coaching indicators (a later, opt-in module) are kept separate
  from any employment-assessment context — one will never inform the other.
- Recommendations are always human-reviewed and human-signed; no automated
  hiring decisions.
- Québec privacy principles (Law 25) guide data-handling design from day one.
